Round 2 · 09 · Reframe

I take home zł a month for about hours of work.

    Why I count in hours

    Złoty are abstract. Hours aren’t. I know exactly what three weeks of meetings, emails and Monday mornings feel like, so when a phone costs three weeks, I can actually weigh it.

    Sometimes the answer is still yes. A week away with people I love is worth a month of work to me, easily. A car loan that eats more than a year is a different conversation. The point isn’t guilt. It’s choosing on purpose.

    It also works the other way. Skipping PPK looks like a small raise. In hours, it’s days of pay you hand back every year.
