Where your salary goes

Put in your monthly take-home pay and give every złoty a job. The drawing shows the split: one line in, a bundle out for each bucket.

zł

What actually lands in your account, after tax and contributions.

50/30/20 is one common starting template: half on needs, 30% on wants, 20% on saving. It's a starting point, not a rule. Here the 20% is split across the three saving buckets.

The flow

Each line is roughly the same slice of your pay. Thicker bundle, more money.

Pay yourself first

Set up standing orders that move the buffer, goals and investing money out on payday, the day your salary lands. What's left in the main account is what you can spend.

If you wait until the end of the month to save what's left, there usually isn't anything left. Not because you're bad with money. Spending just expands to fill the account.

One more thing: PPK comes out before your net pay, so it's not in this picture. If you're in it, you're already saving a bit on top.
