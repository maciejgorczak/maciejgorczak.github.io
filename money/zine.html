Round 2 · 10 · Printable zine

The pocket zine

The whole plan on one sheet of A4. Print it, fold it, cut once. Give it to the friend who keeps saying they’ll sort their money out next month.

Cover

A4, landscape. Print at 100%, no scaling.

The sheet

This is what comes out of the printer. The top row is upside down on purpose. After folding, every page ends up the right way round and in order.

FoldCut
Step 1

Crease it into eight

Fold in half each way, then fold the long strip into quarters. Unfold.

Step 2

Fold in half, cut the middle

Fold the short way. From the folded edge, cut along the centre crease, one panel deep.

Step 3

Fold long, push the ends in

Open it, fold along the long middle, hold both ends and push them together. The cut opens into a diamond.

Step 4

Flatten it into a booklet

Keep pushing until the pages fold flat around the spine. Cover on top. Done.
