# Welcome to My Blog

Welcome! I'm excited to share my thoughts, experiences, and learnings here. This space will serve as a collection of ideas about data, AI, product management, and the intersection of technology and business.

## What to Expect

I plan to write about:

- **Data & AI**: Insights from building data products at scale
- **Product Management**: Lessons learned from leading BI teams
- **Technology**: Tools, frameworks, and methodologies that work
- **Random Thoughts**: Occasional musings on other topics that interest me

## Why Now?

Writing helps clarify thinking. By documenting ideas and experiences, I hope to better understand them myself—and perhaps help others along the way.

Looking forward to this journey.
