# Building Data Products at Scale

Over the past few years, I've been fortunate to work on data products that serve thousands of users—from merchants making business decisions to internal teams optimizing operations. Here are some lessons I've learned.

## Start with the Problem

The biggest mistake in data product development is starting with the data instead of the problem. Users don't care about your elegant data models or sophisticated ML algorithms. They care about making better decisions faster.

Always ask: **What decision does this enable?**

## Embrace Simplicity

Complex dashboards with dozens of metrics rarely get used. The best data products I've built had one clear purpose and a handful of well-chosen metrics.

> "Perfection is achieved not when there is nothing more to add, but when there is nothing left to take away." — Antoine de Saint-Exupéry

## Think About Distribution

A great insight that no one sees is worthless. Think about distribution from day one:

- Where do users naturally work?
- How can you meet them there?
- What's the smallest friction path to value?
